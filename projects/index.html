Helping new projects come to the ASF

Bringing a project to The ASF

The VP OSPO is a visible entry point for organizations and communities considering bringing a project to the Foundation—explaining what the ASF offers and expects, and helping assess whether the ASF is the right home.

  1. 01

    Explore & Assess Fit

    Start a conversation with the OSPO as a visible entry point. Learn what the ASF offers and expects under the Apache Way, and assess whether the ASF is the right home for your project and community.

  2. 02

    Connect with Incubator & PMCs

    Get connected with the Apache Incubator PMC—the main partner for prospective projects—as well as relevant existing Apache PMCs to explore mentorship, alignment, and community readiness.

  3. 03

    Coordinate Onboarding Teams

    Navigate onboarding smoothly through coordinated introductions to Legal Affairs, Brand Management, Infrastructure, and Security so expectations on licensing, trademarks, tooling, and security are clear early.

  4. 04

    Independent Governance Decision

    Project acceptance follows established ASF governance processes. The decision to accept a project remains entirely with the Board—the VP OSPO facilitates and recommends, and never decides or votes by virtue of the role.

Acceptance remains with the Board

The decision to accept a project remains entirely with the Board. The VP OSPO facilitates and recommends, but does not decide or vote on acceptance by virtue of the role.

Helping Apache projects expand their ecosystems

Opt-in ecosystem support for PMCs

PMCs own the technical direction and community decisions of their projects. Support from the OSPO is offered on request or with the PMC's agreement—never imposed.

New contributors, adopters & collaborators

Help PMCs that ask for it to connect with companies, public institutions, and research organizations interested in contributing to or adopting their project.

Cross-project & cross-foundation integrations

Highlight integration opportunities between Apache projects, as well as between Apache projects and projects hosted in other open source foundations.

Real-world adoption feedback

Share adoption stories, needs, and feedback from external organizations directly with the relevant PMCs—while always respecting PMC autonomy.

Joint visibility & events

Work alongside Marketing & Publicity and Conferences to highlight Apache projects and their ecosystems across announcements and events.

Contributor & employer diversity

Encourage broad diversity of contributors and employers within project communities, strengthening long-term community health and resilience.

Are you a PMC looking for new contributors or adopters?

Reach out to the OSPO on discuss@ospo.apache.org or via contact@ospo.apache.org. Introductions happen only with your agreement.
