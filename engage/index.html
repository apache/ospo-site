Primary External Contact

Engaging with The ASF

Whether you represent a software vendor, corporate OSPO, public administration, regulator, research institution, or peer open source foundation, the ASF OSPO connects you with the right people inside the Foundation.

Vendors & Companies Public Sector Peer Foundations & Communities Norms & Standards Routing Directory

Corporate & Commercial

Vendors & Companies

First point of contact for organizations engaging with The ASF or Apache projects.

  • Serve as the first point of contact for companies that want to engage with the ASF or with specific Apache projects.
  • Explain how to participate effectively: contributing as individuals, merit-based governance, licensing and CLAs/CCLAs, trademark usage, and the separation between employers and project roles.
  • Connect companies with the appropriate PMCs and officers (Brand Management, Legal Affairs, Fundraising, Security, etc.), rather than acting on their behalf.
  • Engage with corporate OSPOs on topics such as contribution policies, sustainability, and upstream-first practices.

Government & Policy

Public Sector

Liaison to governments, public administrations, regulators, and public research institutions.

  • Act as the ASF's liaison to governments, public administrations, regulators, and public research institutions.
  • Help public sector organizations understand how they can use, contribute to, and support Apache projects.
  • Coordinate, together with the relevant officers (Public Affairs, Legal Affairs, Security, Privacy, the President and the Board), the ASF's engagement on public policy topics affecting open source, such as cybersecurity regulation, procurement, and digital sovereignty initiatives.
  • Make sure the ASF speaks with one consistent voice in these discussions.

Cross-Foundation

Peer Foundations & Communities

Working relationships across the broader open source ecosystem.

  • Maintain working relationships with peer foundations and open source organizations.
  • Identify opportunities for collaboration: shared tooling, interoperability between projects, joint events, common positions on policy, and shared best practices.
  • Help resolve questions where projects span or depend on multiple foundations.

Standards & Legal

Norms, Standards & Legal Discussions

Participation alongside other ASF Vice Presidents.

  • The VP OSPO is involved, together with other ASF Vice Presidents, in norms, standards, and legal discussions that affect the Foundation and its communities.

Corporate Sponsorship & Donations

Responsible Point of Contact
VP Fundraising

Want to sponsor The Apache Software Foundation or make a financial donation to support ASF infrastructure and operations?

What happens next

The VP OSPO is not a replacement for Fundraising. Sponsorship and donation conversations are handled directly by VP Fundraising, and sponsorship never confers preferential access to projects.

Direct to Security Team

Security Vulnerability Report

Responsible Point of Contact
ASF Security Team

Reporting a security vulnerability in an Apache project? Always follow the official ASF coordinated vulnerability disclosure process directly.

What happens next

The ASF Security Team owns vulnerability handling directly. VP OSPO coordinates with the Security Team only on broader external supply-chain and regulatory security topics.

Direct to Legal / Brand

Legal, Licensing & Trademarks

Responsible Point of Contact
VP Legal Affairs & VP Brand Management

Specific legal, licensing, CLA/CCLA, or Apache trademark and naming inquiries are governed by the Foundation's dedicated officers.

What happens next

VP Legal Affairs owns legal and licensing matters; VP Brand Management owns trademark and naming questions. VP OSPO routes external questions to them rather than duplicating their authority.

ASF Legal & Trademark Policies ↗ Communication Channels
Direct to Marketing / Conferences

Press, Media & Joint Events

Responsible Point of Contact
VP Marketing & Publicity / VP Conferences

Media inquiries, official announcements, press requests, and Community Over Code or joint event inquiries.

What happens next

VP Marketing & Publicity owns public communications and press; VP Conferences owns ASF events. VP OSPO partners with both on ecosystem visibility and joint initiatives.

ASF Newsroom & Press ↗ Communication Channels

Important: what the OSPO does not do

The OSPO does not act on behalf of companies, does not provide preferential access to projects, roadmaps, or decision-makers, and does not replace VP Fundraising for sponsorship conversations. All organizations are treated equally, regardless of size or sponsorship level.
