Reach The ASF OSPO

Contact & Communication Channels

Communication channels for engaging with The Apache Software Foundation's Open Source Program Office—public by default under the Apache Way, with confidentiality when genuinely needed.

Communication channels

How to reach the OSPO

Consistent with the Apache Way, discussions are held on public mailing lists whenever possible. Private channels are used only when confidentiality is genuinely required.

Active Public & Archived

Public Discussion Mailing List

discuss@ospo.apache.org

A public mailing list for general OSPO discussions (discuss@ospo.apache.org), open to the entire Apache community in keeping with the Apache Way.

Active Direct & Confidential

Direct Contact

contact@ospo.apache.org

Direct contact address for external organizations and confidential matters—such as early-stage organizational conversations or sensitive policy matters—with outcomes reported publicly whenever appropriate.

Active Public Web Resource

OSPO Portal

ospo.apache.org

This site (with a direct link from www.apache.org) describing the VP OSPO role, guiding principles, how external organizations can contact the OSPO, and what to expect.

What to expect

When you reach out

  • A timely, consistent response from a single point of contact.
  • Introductions to the right PMCs and officers—never commitments made on their behalf.
  • Equal treatment regardless of organization size, sponsorship level, or commercial relationships.
  • Public discussion by default; outcomes of confidential discussions shared publicly once appropriate.
